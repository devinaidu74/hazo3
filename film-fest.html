1-Minute Film Fest Competition

Welcome to the 1-Minute Film Fest, where compelling storytelling meets concise filmmaking! Challenge yourself to create a captivating short film in 60 seconds or less.

How to Participate:

  • Theme Announcement: A new theme or a set of keywords is provided every Wednesday on the Hazo app.
  • Create & Submit: Produce a short film (maximum 60 seconds, including credits) that incorporates the weekly theme. Submit your film through the Hazo app.
  • Vote & Share: Films are showcased for community voting. Share your masterpiece and encourage friends to vote for you!
  • Winners Announced: Winners are announced every Sunday. The top filmmakers receive grand prizes and recognition within the Hazo community and beyond.

Prizes:

  • 1st Place: A high-end camera drone, Hazo Premium Subscription for 1 year, and a promotional feature on all Hazo platforms.
  • 2nd Place: Advanced video editing software license and Hazo Premium Subscription for 6 months.
  • 3rd Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 3 months.
