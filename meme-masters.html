Meme Masters Competition

Welcome to Meme Masters, where your wit and creativity are celebrated! This weekly competition challenges you to create the most hilarious and relevant memes.

How to Participate:

  • Theme Announcement: A new theme is announced every Monday on the Hazo app and social media.
  • Create & Submit: Design your meme based on the weekly theme. Use your favorite tools and make sure it's original and within community guidelines. Submit your meme directly through the Hazo app.
  • Vote & Share: All submitted memes are open for public voting within the app. Share your entry with friends to get more votes!
  • Winners Announced: Winners are announced every Friday. The top three memes receive exciting prizes and features on Hazo's official channels.

Prizes:

  • 1st Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 1 year, exclusive merchandise, and a featured spot on our homepage.
  • 2nd Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 6 months and Hazo merchandise.
  • 3rd Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 3 months.
