Skit Showdown Competition

Welcome to Skit Showdown, your stage to shine! Showcase your acting, writing, and comedic talents in our weekly skit competition.

How to Participate:

  • Theme Announcement: A new theme or prompt is released every Tuesday on the Hazo app.
  • Create & Upload: Film your original skit (max 3 minutes) based on the theme. Upload your video directly through the Hazo app.
  • Vote & Share: Gather your fans! Skits are open for community voting. The more likes and shares, the better your chances!
  • Winners Announced: Top skits are announced every Saturday. Winners get incredible prizes and recognition from the Hazo community.

Prizes:

  • 1st Place: Professional video equipment package, Hazo Premium Subscription for 1 year, and a featured interview.
  • 2nd Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 6 months and a Hazo merchandise bundle.
  • 3rd Place: Hazo Premium Subscription for 3 months.
