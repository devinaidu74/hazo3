Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hazo, a social media platform that allows users to share and view content. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data is managed responsibly and transparently.

Age Restriction:

We do not allow children under the age of 13 to use our service. Only individuals born after December 31, 2010, are permitted to create accounts.

2. Information We Collect

  • Username
  • First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth
  • Email and Encrypted Password (using bcrypt)
  • Profile Picture
  • Uploaded Posts (images/videos)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data enables us to provide features like authentication, content sharing, and personalization.

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • Posts are visible to all users on the platform.
  • We provide a report system for inappropriate content.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store data securely in MongoDB. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt for maximum security.

  • AWS S3: Used for secure media storage of your uploaded images and videos.
  • AWS Elastic Beanstalk: Hosts our backend infrastructure.
  • Nodemailer: Used for delivering One-Time Passwords (OTP) securely to your email.

5. Your Rights

You have control over your personal data. You can:

  • Request access to your data.
  • Request corrections or updates to your information.
  • Delete your account at any time from the app settings.

For any requests, please contact us at support@hazo.co.in.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party services to provide our platform:

  • AWS S3 for media hosting.
  • AWS Elastic Beanstalk for application hosting.
  • Nodemailer for OTP delivery.

7. Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. We may retain some information as required by law even after account deletion.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Users will be notified via an in-app notification or by email if the changes are significant.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@hazo.co.in.
